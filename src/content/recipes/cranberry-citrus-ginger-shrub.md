### The Science of Cold-Extraction Drinking Vinegars

Classic 18th-century shrubs relied on a 1:1 ratio of sugar to vinegar to preserve fruit juices prior to refrigeration. This modern iteration uses high mechanical shear and natural acidity:
* **Cell Lysis via High Shear:** High-speed blender blades shear the waxy, hydrophobic skin of raw cranberries, releasing concentrated proanthocyanidins and juice without applying heat, which degrades delicate vitamin C.
* **Terpene Extraction in Acetic Acid:** Acetic acid acts as a natural non-polar solvent, pulling aromatic oils (limonene from citrus peel and gingerol from fresh ginger) into stable suspension with a fraction of the sugar found in commercial cordials.
