  - "All prepped mirepoix (onion, carrots, celery, garlic)"
  - "794g no-salt-added crushed tomatoes (1 x 28-oz can, poured on top UNSTIRRED)"
  - "=== TO POST-COOK FINISH (Off-Heat) ==="
  - "284g frozen chopped kale (1 x 10-oz bag)"

recipeInstructions:
  - "Quick-Soak Beans & Prep Board (~28 min total, 5 min hands-on): Place rinsed 400g dry cannellini beans and 8 cups cold water in the 6-qt Instant Pot. Seal lid and cook on High Pressure for 5 minutes, followed by 12-15 minutes Natural Release (vent remaining steam). Meanwhile, prep all Cutting Board items and set out Post-Cook Finish items. Drain beans in a colander, rinse thoroughly, and wipe the pot liner."
  - "Layer Pot (2 min, Zero Browning): Whisk broth, tomato paste, 13.5g EVOO, 9g fine iodized salt, Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, and black pepper smooth in the pot bottom. Crumble raw 93/7 ground turkey directly into the cold broth (20-30 seconds). Nestle in drained quick-soaked beans, spread mirepoix on top, and pour 794g crushed tomatoes evenly over the vegetables. Do not stir."
  - "Pressure Cook (18 min HP + 15 min NR): Lock lid, seal valve, and cook on High Pressure for 18 minutes. Let pressure release naturally for 15 minutes undisturbed so bean and tomato foam settles, then vent any remaining steam and open."
  - "Post-Cook Finish (2-3 min Off-Heat): Immediately stir in 284g frozen chopped kale, 27g EVOO, and 30 mL fresh lemon juice until kale thaws and turns bright emerald."
  - "Portion & Store: Cool uncovered 20-30 minutes before sealing. Refrigerate up to 4 days or freeze up to 3 months in 16-oz deli pints (10 pints at ~440g each with 1/2-inch headspace) or 8 hearty bowls (~550g each)."

A zero-browning, high-potassium Tuscan freezer batch engineered for your 6-quart Instant Pot: quick-soaked dry cannellini beans, cold-broth crumbled `93/7` ground turkey, thick-cut `3/4"` carrots, low-oxalate chopped kale, EVOO, and fresh lemon.
