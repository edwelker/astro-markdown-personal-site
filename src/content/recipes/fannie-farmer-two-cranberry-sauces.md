recipeInstructions:
  - "Boiled Sauce — Dissolve Sugar: Bring 240 mL water and 225-250g sugar to a full boil in a 2-3 qt saucepan over medium-high heat, stirring once to dissolve."
  - "Boiled Sauce — Simmer 10 Minutes: Dump in 340g cranberries, grated ginger, and cardamom. Drop heat to medium, cover (leave a slight crack if foaming), and boil gently for 10 minutes until berries pop and collapse."
  - "Boiled Sauce — Skim & Chill: Pull off heat, skim any pale foam if desired, discard cracked cardamom pods if used, and cool completely so natural cranberry pectin thickens the sauce cleanly."
  - "Raw Relish — Stage 1 Pulse (Citrus & Chili Base): Add quartered unpeeled navel orange, stemmed Fresno or jalapeno, optional fresh ginger, and sugar to the Cuisinart food processor. Pulse in 6-8 short 1-second bursts until finely minced (~1/8-inch bits) with no large bitter peel chunks."
  - "Raw Relish — Stage 2 Pulse (Main Berries): Add 255g (~3/4 bag) raw cranberries. Pulse 6-8 times in short bursts, scraping the bowl wall once halfway, until finely chopped so they release juice to dissolve the sugar."
  - "Raw Relish — Stage 3 Finish (Acid Pops): Add the reserved 85g (~1 cup) whole cranberries on top and give just 2-3 light, quick pulses so they crack into coarse halves and quarters rather than pureeing. Do not run continuously."
  - "Raw Relish — Macerate & Chill: Transfer to a bowl or Mason jar, cover, and refrigerate at least 2-3 hours (ideally overnight) so the sugar dissolves and mellows the raw cranberry bite."

Every Thanksgiving, two complementary bowls cover the entire cranberry spectrum: a **glossy, spoonable boiled sauce** scented with warm ginger and cardamom, alongside a **bright, raw food-processed orange relish** sparked with fresh jalapeño or Fresno chili.

### Original 1896 Base Text (Public Domain)
From Fannie Merritt Farmer, *The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book* (Boston: Little, Brown, and Company, 1896 — [Project Gutenberg #65061](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/65061) / [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/bostoncookingsch00farm_0)):

> *"Pick over and wash three cups cranberries. Put in a stewpan, add one and one-fourth cups sugar and one cup boiling water. Cover, and boil ten minutes. Care must be taken that they do not boil over. Skim and cool."*

### Optional Upgrades (After Dialing In the Base)
* **Boiled Sauce:** Bloom thin ginger coins, `3–4` cracked green cardamom pods, and a wide strip of orange peel in the boiling liquid for `3 min` before adding berries; swap half the water (`120 mL`) for apple cider or fresh orange juice; finish off-heat with `1/8 tsp` fine iodized salt.
* **Raw Relish:** Add `1/4 tsp` fine salt and `1 tbsp` fresh lime juice or apple cider vinegar during Stage 1 to speed up maceration and keep the chili bright against rich gravy.
