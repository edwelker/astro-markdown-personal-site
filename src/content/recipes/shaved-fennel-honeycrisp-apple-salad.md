Built for Thanksgiving travel and zero host-oven competition: paper-thin mandoline-shaved fennel and diagonal-sliced celery stay aggressively crisp for hours with zero wilting, then come together in two minutes right before dinner with Honeycrisp apples, toasted walnuts, salty shaved Pecorino, and a bright apple-cider Dijon vinaigrette.

### Make-Ahead & Travel Packing
* **Night Before or Morning Of:** Shake the vinaigrette in a Mason jar, toast and chop the walnuts, shave the fennel and celery (store with a damp paper towel), and peel Pecorino ribbons.
* **At the Host's Table:** Slice the Honeycrisp apples fresh (or bring pre-sliced apples tossed with `1 tbsp` fresh lemon juice), dump, dress, and toss in 2 minutes.
