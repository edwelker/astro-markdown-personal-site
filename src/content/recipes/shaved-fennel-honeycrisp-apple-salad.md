Built for crisp texture and zero host-oven competition: `45°` mandoline-shaved fennel crescents and bias-sliced celery stay aggressively crisp for hours with zero wilting, then come together in two minutes right before dinner with knife-cut Honeycrisp apple batons, two-texture toasted walnuts, dual-cut Aged Pecorino, and a bright apple-cider Dijon vinaigrette.

### Make-Ahead & Travel Packing
* **Night Before or Morning Of:** Shake the vinaigrette in a Mason jar, toast and 75/25 split the walnuts, shave the fennel (45° bias) and celery (store with a damp paper towel), and prep the Microplaned + flaked Pecorino.
* **At the Table:** Pat fennel/celery dry if damp, cut the Honeycrisp apple into `1/4-in` batons (or bring pre-cut batons tossed with `1.5 tsp` lemon juice), dump, dress, and toss in 2 minutes.

### Iteration Log: Attempt 1 → Attempt 2 Refinements
* **Halved Yield (`8–10` $\rightarrow$ `4–5` servings):** Original batch was way too large; scaled to a tighter side-dish / personal-meal batch.
* **Fennel at `45°` with Core Intact (`1.5–2 mm`):** Slicing parallel to the base produced wide flat slabs; keeping the woody core intact as a handle while shaving at `45°` produces thin, lacy crescents.
* **Apple Batons Instead of Mandoline:** Paper-thin wet mandoline slices suction-stuck together; sturdy knife-cut `1/4-in` batons (`1.5–2 in` long) tangle with the fennel and never clump or sink like dice.
* **Walnuts Bumped & `75/25` Split (`2/3 cup`):** Coarsely chopped chunks (`6–9 mm`) + `~25%` fine crushed rubble (`1–3 mm`) disperse walnut bite throughout every forkful instead of only halves.
* **Dual-Cut Pecorino & Whole Herb Tufts:** Microplaning `1/3` of the Pecorino (using the same Microplane already out for lemon zest) thickens and seasons the `1.2:1` vinaigrette film so it clings instead of pooling; whole `1–1.5 in` herb tufts stay bright and airy.
