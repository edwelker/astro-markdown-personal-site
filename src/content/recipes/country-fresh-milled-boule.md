### The Three Baking Techniques

1. **50% Fresh-Milled Grain Blend at 82.5% Hydration:** Combining strong Hard Red Winter Wheat with moisture-retaining Rye pentosans and extensible Spelt—and bumping hydration to 82.5%—keeps the crumb open and tender despite 50% whole-grain bran.
2. **48–72 Hour Cold Cure (Phytase & Organic Acids):** Extended cold fermentation activates cereal phytase to degrade phytic acid (unlocking iron, zinc, and magnesium) while building lactic and acetic acids that blunt post-meal glucose spikes.
3. **Pre-Slice, Freeze & Toast (RS3):** Freezing 50g slices and toasting straight from frozen recrystallizes starch into Type 3 Resistant Starch (~2.4g fiber and 109 kcal per 50g slice).
4. **Role & Storage:** Best **2.5–3h pre-ride** (Slider 1–2) and **legume/bean-chili safe** (zero dairy). Pair 2-slice meals with a 1,200-step stroll (or 10–15 min soleus pushups) + salad/greens for Vitamin C/K1/nitrates. Store intact grain berries in airtight Gamma Seal buckets/jars and freeze any open bagged flour over humid summer months.
