recipeInstructions:
  - "NutriMill Impact Batch (Setting 2, 1 Pass): Weigh the 5 NutriMill grains (270g hard red wheat, 72g dark rye, 48g spelt, 18g pre-washed quinoa, 12g amaranth) into one container. Turn the NutriMill Impact on to Setting 2 first (finer bran slices less gluten than Setting 3), then pour all 5 grains into the hopper together and mill in ONE pass. Empty into your mixing bowl and let cool 15-20 minutes if warm to the touch."
  - "Spice Grinder Batch: Weigh 30g rolled oats into your spice grinder, pulse ~10 seconds into a coarse flour, and dump into the mixing bowl."
  - "Mix in Mixing Bowl (1 Minute): Whisk 150g bread flour, optional 15-20g vital wheat gluten, 12g salt, and 0.8g yeast into the milled flours. Pour in 460g (475g with VWG) fridge-cold water and stir ~1 minute until no dry pockets remain."
  - "Counter Bulk (8-10 Hours): Cover the bowl and leave on the counter (68-70°F) until puffy and bubbly on top. Fresh-milled flour ferments ~30-50% faster than bagged flour—do not let bulk overshoot past 10 hours."
  - "Pre-Shape Fridge Chill (1-2 Hours — Easy Handling): Move the covered mixing bowl straight into the fridge for 1-2 hours BEFORE first shaping. Cold dough firms up hydrated starches so high-whole-grain dough turns out cleanly, stops sticking, and holds tight surface tension effortlessly."
  - "Shape Cold & Cold Proof (12-24 Hours Total Fridge Time Max): Turn chilled dough onto a lightly floured counter, fold edges into a tight round ball, and place seam-side UP in a snug 7.5-8 inch bowl lined with a floured cotton towel. Cover and refrigerate 12-24 hours total (hard cap—leaving 75% fresh-milled dough 48 hours lets rye/spelt proteases degrade the gluten and flatten oven spring)."
  - "Bake in 5-Qt Dutch Oven (450°F -> 425°F): Preheat your 5-qt Tramontina Dutch oven (metal knob) + lid at 450°F for 35-45 minutes. Flip cold dough onto fresh dry parchment, slash the top, cover, and bake 25 minutes at 450°F. Remove lid, drop oven to 425°F, and bake 18-22 minutes until deep chestnut-brown (208-210°F inside)."
  - "Cool & Freeze: Cool 2.5-3 hours on a wire rack before slicing into ~50g slices; freeze half for quick toasting."

A rustic Dutch-oven loaf milled fresh at **75% whole grains and oats** (`45%` hard red wheat, `12%` dark rye, `8%` spelt, `5%` rolled oats, `3%` quinoa, `2%` amaranth) paired with **25% high-protein bread flour** (and optional Vital Wheat Gluten) for reliable oven spring.

### Why This Formula Works
* **75% Whole-Grain Micronutrients & Complete Protein:** Milling fresh keeps intact magnesium, zinc, iron, and B-vitamins that refined flour strips away. **Quinoa and amaranth** supply the lysine wheat lacks, while **rolled oats** add soluble beta-glucan fiber for fullness.
* **Better Mineral Absorption & Slower Blood Sugar:** A **`12–24 hour` cold fridge ferment** lets the `12%` dark rye's natural phytase break down phytic acid (unlocking minerals) while building tangy flavor—without letting fresh-milled rye/spelt proteases dissolve the gluten network. Slicing to `50g`, freezing half, and toasting from frozen creates **Type 3 Resistant Starch (RS3)** for steadier blood sugar and lasting satiety.
