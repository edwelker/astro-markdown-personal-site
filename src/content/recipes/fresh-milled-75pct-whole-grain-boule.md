recipeInstructions:
  - "Mill Fresh Grains: Set your countertop mill to a fine bread setting and mill the hard red wheat (270g), dark rye (72g), spelt (48g), oats (30g), quinoa (18g), and amaranth (12g) directly into your mixing bowl while warm and aromatic."
  - "Whisk Dry Ingredients: Add the 150g bread flour, 12g sea salt, and 2g instant yeast; whisk thoroughly for 20-30 seconds until uniformly blended."
  - "Mix Dough: Pour in 480g room-temp water and stir with a wooden spoon or wet hand for ~1 minute until all dry pockets disappear into a shaggy, sticky dough."
  - "Bulk Ferment (12-18 Hours): Cover tightly and rest at room temperature (68-72°F) until doubled, domed, and speckled with surface bubbles."
  - "Shape & Cold Retard (48-72 Hours): Scrape dough onto a lightly floured surface, gently fold edges toward the center into a taut round boule, and place seam-side down on parchment inside a bowl or banneton. Cover and refrigerate 48-72 hours."
  - "Bake Covered, Then Crisp: Preheat oven with the empty 7-qt Dutch oven and lid inside at 475°F (245°C) for 45 minutes. Lower cold dough on parchment into the hot pot, slash top 1/2 inch deep, cover, and bake 30 minutes. Remove lid, drop oven to 450°F, and bake 15-20 minutes until deep chestnut-mahogany and internal temp reaches 205-210°F."
  - "Cool Completely: Rest on a wire rack 2+ hours before slicing so the crumb sets cleanly."

A rustic, high-flavor 7-qt Dutch-oven loaf milled fresh at **75% whole grains and seeds** (`45%` hard red wheat, `12%` dark rye, `8%` spelt, `5%` rolled oats, `3%` quinoa, `2%` amaranth) paired with **25% high-protein bread flour** for reliable oven spring.

### Why This Formula Works
* **48–72h Cold Retard:** Activates dark rye's natural phytase enzymes to break down `~50–70%` of phytic acid (unlocking magnesium, zinc, and iron) while building deep, malty sourdough-like tang.
* **Complete Amino-Acid Complementarity:** Quinoa and amaranth supply lysine lacking in wheat, rye, and spelt without turning the crumb gummy.
* **Slice, Freeze & Toast (RS3):** Weighing `50g` slices and freezing half the cooled loaf prevents warm-bread grazing and creates Type 3 Resistant Starch when toasted straight from frozen.

### Nutrition (Per `50g` Measured Slice — 19 Slices Total)
* **110 kcal** | **4.2g Protein** | **22.7g Total Carbs** (**2.9g Fiber** / 19.8g Net) | **0.7g Fat**

### Portioning & Storage
* Weigh and slice the cooled loaf into **19 uniform `50g` slices**; freeze at least half immediately with parchment squares between slices and toast straight from frozen.
