A rustic, high-flavor 7-qt Dutch-oven loaf milled fresh at **75% whole grains and seeds** (`45%` hard red wheat, `12%` dark rye, `8%` spelt, `5%` rolled oats, `3%` quinoa, `2%` amaranth) paired with **25% high-protein bread flour** for reliable oven spring.

### Why This Formula Works
* **48–72h Cold Retard:** Activates dark rye's natural phytase enzymes to break down `~50–70%` of phytic acid (unlocking magnesium, zinc, and iron) while building deep, malty sourdough-like tang.
* **Complete Amino-Acid Complementarity:** Quinoa and amaranth supply lysine lacking in wheat, rye, and spelt without turning the crumb gummy.
* **Slice, Freeze & Toast (RS3):** Weighing `50g` slices and freezing half the cooled loaf prevents warm-bread grazing and creates Type 3 Resistant Starch when toasted straight from frozen.

### Hardware
* Countertop grain mill
* Kitchen scale (grams)
* Large mixing bowl + parchment paper
* **7-qt Dutch oven** with lid

### Instructions
1. **Mill Fresh Grains:** Set your countertop mill to a fine bread setting and mill the hard red wheat (`270g`), dark rye (`72g`), spelt (`48g`), oats (`30g`), quinoa (`18g`), and amaranth (`12g`) directly into your mixing bowl while warm and aromatic.
2. **Whisk Dry Ingredients:** Add the `150g` bread flour, `12g` sea salt, and `2g` instant yeast; whisk thoroughly for `20–30 sec` until uniformly blended.
3. **Mix Dough:** Pour in `480g` room-temp water and stir with a wooden spoon or wet hand for `~1 min` until all dry pockets disappear into a shaggy, sticky dough.
4. **Bulk Ferment (`12–18 Hours`):** Cover tightly and rest at room temperature (`68–72°F`) until doubled, domed, and speckled with surface bubbles.
5. **Shape & Cold Retard (`48–72 Hours`):** Scrape dough onto a lightly floured surface, gently fold edges toward the center into a taut round boule, and place seam-side down on parchment inside a bowl or banneton. Cover and refrigerate **`48–72 hours`**.
6. **Bake Covered, Then Crisp:** Preheat your oven with the **empty 7-qt Dutch oven and lid inside at `475°F` (`245°C`)** for `45 min`. Lower the cold dough on its parchment into the hot pot, slash the top `1/2 inch` deep, cover, and bake **`30 min`**. Remove the lid, drop oven to **`450°F`**, and bake **`15–20 min`** until deep chestnut-mahogany and internal temp reaches **`205–210°F`**.
7. **Cool Completely:** Rest on a wire rack **`2+ hours`** before slicing so the crumb sets cleanly.

### Portioning & Storage
* Weigh and slice the cooled loaf into **19 uniform `50g` slices**; freeze at least half immediately with parchment squares between slices and toast straight from frozen.
