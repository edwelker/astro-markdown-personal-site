### Why Whole Citrus Peel & Pith Matters

Leaving the entire peel and white albedo pith on the orange is essential:
* **Natural Terpenes:** Pulsing the cold orange peel ruptures citrus oil glands, releasing aromatic limonene that emulsifies directly with the tart cranberry juices.
* **Soluble Pectin Network:** The white albedo pith supplies abundant soluble citrus pectin. Paired with a pinch of salt and a modest dash of maple syrup, the pectin draws moisture from the ruptured berries and sets into a glossy, ruby-red gel over 12 hours without any boiling or commercial gelatin.
* **Versatile Uses:** Beyond pairing with roasted poultry or pork, spoon 2 tablespoons into the bottom of a tall glass, add ice, and top with high-fizz seltzer for an instant tart shrub spritz.
