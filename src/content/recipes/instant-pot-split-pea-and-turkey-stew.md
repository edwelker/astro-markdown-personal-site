### Batch Volume & Instant Pot Safety

This recipe is calibrated precisely for the operational geometry of a **6-quart Instant Pot**:
* **Liquid Ceiling:** The 8.5 cups of broth combined with the aromatics and legumes brings the static level to approximately 1.5 inches below the 2/3 maximum fill line.
* **The Whisk Emulsion:** Because red lentils disintegrate rapidly under 15 psi while green split peas soften, a brisk 45-second whisking produces an immediate creamy potage without requiring an immersion blender or cornstarch.
* **The Acid Brightener:** Legumes and lean poultry carry deep earthy flavors; stirring 1 tablespoon of apple cider vinegar (or fresh lemon juice) directly into your bowl just before eating lifts the flavor without thinning the velvety stew base.
