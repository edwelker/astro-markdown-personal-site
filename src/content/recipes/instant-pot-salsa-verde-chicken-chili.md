### The Three Culinary Techniques

1. **The Salted 5-Minute Quick-Soak:** Pressure-cooking dried pinto and white beans for 5 minutes in lightly salted water (1 tsp kosher salt per 6 cups) softens the bean skins evenly and flushes out gas-producing oligosaccharides when drained—giving you tender, intact beans in 25 minutes instead of an overnight soak.
2. **The Layered "Do Not Stir" Build:** Jarred roasted tomatillo salsa verde is thick enough to scorch against the Instant Pot's bottom heating element if stirred in early. Layering beans, aromatics, chicken, and plain water underneath—and pouring the salsa verde last on top—eliminates `BURN` errors completely.
3. **The White-Bean Mash Emulsion:** Instead of using flour, cornstarch, or heavy cream, pressing 1 cup of the cooked Great Northern/Cannellini beans against the pot wall releases natural bean starch that turns the bright tomatillo broth into a rich, velvety chili.
