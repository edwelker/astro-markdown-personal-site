### The Four Culinary Techniques

1. **The Oil-Bloomed Spice & Bouillon Base:** Tomatillo salsa + plain water + skinless chicken thighs can taste flat and one-dimensionally sour. Blooming cumin, Mexican oregano, smoked paprika, and chipotle in 1 tbsp olive oil extracts their fat-soluble aromatics, while Chicken Better Than Bouillon supplies the savory glutamates needed to balance the tomatillo acid.
2. **The Salted 5-Minute Quick-Soak:** Pressure-cooking 2.5 cups of dried pinto and white beans for 5 minutes in lightly salted water (1.5 tsp kosher salt per 8 cups) softens the skins evenly and flushes out gas-producing oligosaccharides—giving you a hearty, bean-dense chili without an overnight soak.
3. **The Layered "Do Not Stir" Build:** Keeping the thick Herdez Salsa Verde on top of the chicken and bouillon base prevents it from scorching on the bottom heating element before the pot comes to pressure.
4. **The Masa Harina Emulsion:** Whisking 1/4 cup Masa Harina into cold water and simmering for 3–5 minutes at the end gelatinizes the nixtamalized corn starch, binding free water into a thick, velvety broth while adding subtle toasted-corn flavor for just ~18 calories per bowl.
