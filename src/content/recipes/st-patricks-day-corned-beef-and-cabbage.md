### Temperature Target
190F is the absolute target internal temperature for the meat, not the water. This is the exact temperature where tough collagen melts into gelatin. The water itself should stay around 200F to provide a gentle, steady heat transfer.

### The Veggie Swap
Cooking the vegetables in the residual broth after pulling the meat prevents the potatoes from turning to mush during the long meat-cooking phase. 

### Flavor Adjustments
Serve with a good fatty sour cream (like Fage, or any Eastern European [Polish, Russian, etc.]) on the side to balance the heavy salt and earthiness of the broth.
