### The Three Baking Techniques

1. **Scalded Oat & Flax Bound-Water Matrix:** Gelatinizing rolled oats, cracked rye, and ground flax into a thick porridge *before* folding into the dough traps moisture inside the crumb, keeping slices soft for 5 days and slowing gastric emptying.
2. **Sturdy Base Dough Ratio:** Holding the base dough at 71.4% hydration keeps the gluten network strong enough to support the wet porridge without slumping.
3. **Omega-3 & Soluble Fiber Boost:** Freezer-stored whole flaxseed (ground fresh right before scalding to prevent oil oxidation) adds ~1.4g plant Omega-3 ALA per two slices while dropping caloric density to ~106 kcal per 50g slice.
4. **Role & Timing:** Best for **Slider 0 (Rest)** & **post-ride** (highest Marciani satiety; legume/bean-chili safe). Skip `<2.5h` pre-ride to avoid sloshing; pair 2-slice meals with a 1,200-step stroll or soleus pushups + salad/greens.
