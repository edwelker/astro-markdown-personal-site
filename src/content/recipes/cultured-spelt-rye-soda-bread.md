### The Three Baking Techniques

1. **70% Tender Spelt-Rye Grist:** Fresh-milled spelt delivers soft, nutty Irish brown-bread chew without hard-wheat heaviness, balanced with 2 tbsp caraway seeds.
2. **0% Greek Yogurt + Buttermilk Leavening:** Replacing half the buttermilk with 0% Greek yogurt adds +22g protein across the loaf and extra lactic acid to completely neutralize 1 tsp (5g) baking soda with zero added butter or oil.
3. **Zero-Rest Hot Skillet Lift:** Mixing room-temp cultured dairy into a preheated 425F covered Lodge chicken fryer triggers immediate CO2 rise before the crust sets.
4. **Role & Iron/Calcium Partitioning:** **Slider 0 (Rest) & post-ride only** (avoid `<4h` pre-ride due to unfermented fructans). **Pair with sardines, eggs, or clam chowder — partition away from bean chili** (dairy calcium + unfermented phytates block non-heme iron). Pair 2-slice meals with a 1,200-step stroll or soleus pushups + salad/greens.
