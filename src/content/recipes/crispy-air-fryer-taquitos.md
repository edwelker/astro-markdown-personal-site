### Why This Recipe Works for Everyone

Commercial frozen taquitos (Delimex, El Monterey) rely on industrial deep-fryers, scrap meat, and modified starches to survive months of freezer storage. Homemade recipes often swing to the opposite extreme: spending three hours braising, reducing sauces, and dirtying five pans.

This recipe hits the sweet spot: **standard 6-inch grocery tortillas, real charred chicken thighs, home-cooked beans, and fewer rolls to make (16–18 count batch) for maximum weeknight return.**

### Poultry Cooking Options (Choose Your Preferred Method)

1. **The Skillet-Charred Thighs (Flagship):**
   * Season 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs with the spice blend. Sear in a screaming-hot cast-iron skillet with 1 tsp oil for 5–6 minutes per side until charred edges form. Rest 5 minutes, then chop finely. Delivers outdoor grill flavor inside the crunchy shell.
2. **The 6-Minute Skillet (Ground Turkey or Chicken):**
   * Brown 1 lb 93/7 ground turkey in a skillet with the spices for 5–6 minutes. Fast, uniform, and rolls tightly.
3. **The Instant Pot Salsa Poultry (Hands-Off):**
   * Place 1.5 lbs chicken thighs or breasts in the Instant Pot with 1/2 cup salsa and the spices. Cook on High Pressure for 12 minutes, quick-release, and shred.
4. **The Store Shortcut (For Family & Friends):**
   * While you cook fresh, friends or family can easily swap in 3 cups of shredded warm supermarket rotisserie chicken to make this zero-cook.

### The Dried Bean Protocol (No-Soak / Quick-Soak in the Instant Pot)

Rather than paying for canned beans, use dried pinto or black beans from your pantry:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               THE INSTANT POT DRIED BEAN METHOD (NO SOAKING REQUIRED)                  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. RINSE: Add 1.5 cups (~300g) dry pinto or black beans to the Instant Pot.            │
│    Add water until it sits 1 inch above the beans.                                     │
│ 2. 5-MINUTE QUICK-SOAK: High Pressure 5 min → Natural Release 15 min.                  │
│    Drain and rinse beans thoroughly. (This leaches out water-soluble gas-causing       │
│    oligosaccharides and pre-hydrates the seed coat).                                   │
│ 3. MAIN COOK: Return beans to pot with 4 cups fresh water, 1 tsp salt, and 1 bay leaf. │
│    Pressure cook on High for 22 minutes (Pinto) or 25 minutes (Black), followed by     │
│    a 15-minute Natural Release.                                                        │
│ 4. YIELD: Yields ~4.5 cups of tender, creamy beans. Measure 1 cup for your taquito     │
│    batch, and freeze the rest in pint deli containers for future meals!                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
*(Quick Alternative: 1 standard 15-oz can of black or pinto beans, rinsed and drained, works seamlessly if you're out of batch beans).*

### Rolling & Tortilla Flexibility (Corn vs. Flour)

* **Standard 6-inch Corn Tortillas (Traditional Brittle Crunch):**
  * *The Anti-Cracking Rule:* Cold corn tortillas will split. Stack 8–9 tortillas between two damp paper towels and microwave on High for 50–60 seconds until steaming hot and floppy as fabric. Work from inside the warm towel.
* **Standard 6-inch Thin Flour Tortillas (Puffy Flauta Crunch):**
  * Flour tortillas feature wheat gluten elasticity—they roll cold with zero cracking risk and air-fry into a blistered, pastry-crisp shell. Warm for 15 seconds just to make them soft.
* **The Seam-Down Lock:**
  * No toothpicks required. The combination of 2 oz cream cheese (which stops melted Jack cheese from separating) and placing the taquito seam-side down on the preheated rack seals the roll within the first 2 minutes of cooking.

### Cooking Across Different Appliances

Always place taquitos **seam-side down** and mist the tops lightly with oil:

| Equipment | Temperature | Time from Frozen | Time if Fresh | Kitchen Cue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Air Fryer Basket** | **400°F (204°C)** | **10–12 mins** | 7–9 mins | Shake or roll once at minute 6. Tips sizzle. |
| **Toaster Oven Air Fryer** | **380°F (193°C)** | **11–13 mins** | 8–10 mins | Middle rack avoids scorching before center melts. |
| **Standard Big Oven** | **425°F (218°C)** | **18–22 mins** | 15–18 mins | Bake on a foil/parchment sheet; flip once at minute 10. |

### The 30-Second Greek Yogurt Crema
Whisk **1 cup plain 0% Greek yogurt** with **2 tablespoons salsa**, a squeeze of fresh lime juice, and a pinch of garlic salt. Dipping hot crunchy taquitos into cold tangy crema provides an irresistible temperature contrast and adds **15g of complete protein** with zero guilt.
