Joe’s warned me about scaring everybody… just trying to help people learn from my mistakes.  That said… I admit that I’ve probably kept too many surprises secret.  Here’s why you should actually be excited:

This is the prettiest tour that you can imagine.  We go from the industrial and straight-forward GAP, which is the re-imagining of a working-class rail trail, which has some gorgeous views of various rivers along the way, clean tall wide and straight tree-lined paths, walled with flowers and blasted rock with trickles of water cascading down… all while retaining the aura of it’s industrial heritage…

To the C&O (yea, I’m biased, it looks like my backyard) which is the total opposite, a tow-path meant for humans.  Much smaller, closer, warmer, and like life, sometimes a little messy.  Filled with wise old trees and a sweet tree aroma, all sorts of animals (deer, Great Blue Herons, turtles, snakes, fish, and even a Bald Eagle on the last day), beautiful flowers (it’s Black-Eye’d Susan season, MD’s state flower), and and a feeling of equilibrium, since nature and the towpath have had at least 150 years to balance.  Plus the constant whispers of history (Harpers Ferry/John Brown, Antietam, etc.).   And then camp will be filled with fireflies, rose colored sunsets (as above), and grass that’s cool to your toe’s touch.

^^^^ is why I’m doing it for the 4th time. 🙂 ❤️
